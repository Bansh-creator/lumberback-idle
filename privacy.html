Lumberback Idle: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the Lumberback Idle game. It explains what the game does and doesn’t do with your information.

No accounts

Lumberback Idle has no accounts, logins or sign-ups.

No personal data collected

The game does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Your progress stays on your device

Your game progress (and settings such as sound on/off) is saved locally on your device only. It is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. The RESTART button in Settings also deletes it.

No ads, no analytics

Version 1 of Lumberback Idle has no ads and no analytics or tracking.

The "Report a bug" button

When you tap Report a bug in Settings, the game copies some basic device and game info to your clipboard: the game version, your current island, your in-game cash, your device's browser user-agent and the time. It does this only when you tap the button. Nothing is sent automatically. You decide whether to paste that info anywhere.

External links

The game links to our Discord community. If you open it, you leave the game, and Discord's own terms and privacy policy apply to whatever you do there.

Children

Lumberback Idle does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
